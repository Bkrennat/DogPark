About this event

Join our professional dog training class designed for dogs of all ages and skill levels. This 90-minute session covers essential commands, behavior correction, and bonding techniques to strengthen the relationship with your furry friend. Perfect for both new puppy owners and experienced dog parents looking to refine their dog's behavior.

What you'll learn
  • Basic commands (sit, stay, come, heel)
  • Leash walking techniques
  • Positive reinforcement methods
  • Common behavior problem solutions
Requirements
  • All dogs must be vaccinated
  • Dogs must be at least 4 months old
  • Bring a leash and treats
  • Aggressive dogs not permitted
